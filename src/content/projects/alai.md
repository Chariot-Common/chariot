## The brief

Alai is a YC-backed (W24) AI presentation tool that helps tech professionals make high-quality decks faster. They asked us for a fresh, intuitive v1 that stands out from the sea of tools already on the market.

## What we did

Product design for the whole platform: creating a deck from scratch, editing individual slides and elements, and presenting. Alongside it, an AI UX strategy so people get relevant output the first time, a UI and design system that feels clean and focused, and a theming and canvas engine that is flexible but deceptively simple.

## What changed

Most AI tools promise magic but need expertise to get it. We designed around that blank-start problem, so the first deck Alai makes already feels like the magic it promises.
