## The brief

Skyway makes fitness-first food that satisfies cravings. With the branding and packaging done, they needed a website that balances conversions, the brand story and an experience people enjoy.

## What we did

Conversion-focused D2C UX that is sleek and mobile-first, keeping decisions to a minimum. Content that explains nutrition, ingredients and the brand story in an engaging way. Visual and interaction design that carries the brand's rich language onto the web, tuned for the main way people arrive: Instagram and X.

## What changed

Three clicks to checkout. And full transparency on ingredients, so visitors know not just what goes into a bottle of Skyway, but why it's there.
