tagline: Design for India's tastiest guilt-free cravings solution
overview:
  - Skyway is an ambitious, fitness first, craving solution that makes tasty food to get you closer to your fitness goals.
  - After the branding and packaging design was completed, we hopped aboard to create their website — one that smoothly balances optimising for conversions, presenting the brand story, and creating an engaging experience.
services:
  - Conversion-optimised UX for D2C eCommerce that's sleek and mobile-optimised, minimising the decisions users have to take.
  - Website content that differentiates the brand from other players and presents the nutrition details, ingredients and brand story in an engaging way.
  - Visual & interaction design that translates and extends the rich brand visual language for the dynamic nature of web.
  - Mobile-first design that optimises for the primary user acquisition funnel — social media platforms like Instagram and X (Twitter).
