## The brief

Since 2016, The Ken has grown into India's foremost premium business publication, and its offering has grown with it. They asked us to reimagine the entire platform from the ground up: premium, handcrafted and meticulously thought through.

## What we did

A visual language that positions The Ken as premium, credible journalism. Experience design that improves reading, discovery and engagement. A subscription flow that shows the value clearly and converts. And a design system spanning mobile and desktop web, iOS, Android, iPadOS and email, followed by a year and a half of design sprints across sub-products and features.

## What changed

Higher engagement from better discovery, higher average order values from a rebuilt pricing page, more newsletter engagement, and a design system that lets the team ship new launches quickly.
