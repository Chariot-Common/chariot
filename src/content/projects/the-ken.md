tagline: Redefining India's foremost, premium business publication from the ground up
overview:
  - Since their inception in 2016, The Ken has grown to become India's foremost premium business publication. Over the years, their offerings and positioning have evolved too.
  - They partnered with us to completely reimagine their entire platform towards this evolution — premium, handcrafted, and meticulously thought through, from the ground up.
services:
  - Visual language & design that positions the product as a premium, credible, and distinguished business journalism platform.
  - Experience design that's crisp, thoughtful and delightful, improving the reading experience, content discoverability and user engagement.
  - Subscription flow strategy & design that presents The Ken's value in a visually compelling manner and optimises for conversions.
  - Multi-platform design system that spans across mobile & desktop web, the iOS app, Android app, iPadOS app and emails.
  - Continued design sprints over a year and a half across multiple sub-products, features and deeper product experiences.

  - kicker: Solving business problems with design innovation
    heading: "Designing beyond comparison: framing value to drive results"
    text:
      - Comparing features between pricing tiers matters for functional products. For a premium product like The Ken, we deduced that showcasing the value of subscribing instead would have a much better impact. And it turned out to be correct! The result — a meaningful uplift in average order value.
    media:
      - { src: https://framerusercontent.com/images/hUQAmV2BnIrKwaxB0rtHldl7E.png, alt: "The pricing page: the only business subscription you need" }

  - heading: Increasing stories read per session via improved content discoverability
    text:
      - Via analytics, we observed that most users were landing directly on the story pages first. So how do we show them the meticulously curated content on the home page? Simple — we put the home page at the end of the story!
      - We also created a system of units to contextually prompt to other relevant stories and content on The Ken.
    media:
